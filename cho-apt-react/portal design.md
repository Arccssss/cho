# Samotial Health Portal - Design Summary

## Layout & Structure
The website features a **clean, hierarchical layout** with:
- Dark navy hero section at top with left-aligned text content and right-aligned product image
- White/light grey body sections below for feature cards and information blocks
- Dark navy footer section at bottom

## Color Palette
- **Primary (#002046)**: Deep navy blue used for headers, backgrounds, and CTAs
- **Secondary (#fafdfd)**: Near-white, used for text on dark backgrounds and card backgrounds
- **Tertiary (#e3e2e6)**: Soft grey for subtle dividers and secondary backgrounds
- **Light Blue (#d0e1fb)**: Accent blue for icons and highlights

## Key Design Elements

### Hero Section
- White headline: "Modern Public Health for Every Citizen"
- Subtext describing system benefits
- Primary CTA button ("Schedule Your Consultation Now")

### Feature Cards ("Why Choose Our System?")
- 6 icon-based feature blocks in 3x2 grid
- icons (use react-icons)
- Titles + descriptive text below each
- Features include:
  - **Verified Health Data**: Secure and standardized health records accessible by authorized users
  - **Real-time Scheduling**: Find availability for all clinic health centers and book appointments
  - **Unified History**: Access your complete and consolidated health history from all providers
  - **Community Driven**: Designed with patient input to address real user accessibility needs
  - **Smart Reminders**: Machine-validated alerts and appointment notifications for upcoming visits
  - **Integrated Services**: Connect seamlessly with laboratory, health, pharmacy services

### Information Section
- Two-column layout with grey background
- **"Before You Book"** (left): Details about scheduling and booking procedures
- **"Need Help?"** (right): Support contact information and assistance options
- Contact details and support links

### CTA Section
- Dark navy banner with white text
- Headline: "Ready to get started?"
- Subtext about deployment and documentation
- Dual CTAs:
  - Primary button: "Schedule your appointment"

## Typography & Visual Hierarchy
- Large, bold headlines in white on dark backgrounds
- Smaller body text for descriptions
- Icon-based visual identification for features
- Clean sans-serif typography throughout
- High contrast text for accessibility

## Visual Identity
- **Overall Aesthetic**: Modern, professional healthcare portal with trust-focused design
- **Key Symbols**: health icons emphasizing security and verified data
- **Spacing**: Generous padding and whitespace for clarity
- **Icons**: Simple, flat design with light blue accent color
- **Imagery**: Professional healthcare facility photos

## Navigation
- Top navigation bar with sections: Features, Testimonials, Audit
- Logo/branding on left

## Design Philosophy
- Accessibility-conscious (color contrast, clear typography)
- User-centered (feature cards highlight patient benefits)
- Professional yet approachable tone
